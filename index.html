Questions

Before you sign up

Does it scrape Yelp or Google for me?

No. You bring the list by pasting page text, running a map search or loading a file. Hunting Desk organizes, scores and drafts from there.

Can it tell if a number is on WhatsApp?

Not from a number alone. It looks for WhatsApp links on a business's own website, and you can mark a lead after you test it yourself.

Who can see my leads?

Only you. Every account is separate, and a new account starts empty.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. The layout adapts to phone and desktop, and you can pick a light or dark look and an accent color under Appearance.

Search Yelp for the businesses you want to sell websites to (plumbers, barbers, dentists), not for web designers. Paste the search link, open it, copy the results page text, and paste that text below. Each Yelp page lists about 10 businesses.

Option 1 · Google Places API

Returns name, phone, website, rating and reviews directly. You need an API key with Places API (New) enabled and a billing account in Google Cloud. Restrict the key to your site's address. This option works on the deployed site, not in the Claude preview.

Option 2 · Paste from Google Maps, no key

Press "Open this search in Google Maps" above, scroll the results list so the businesses load, press Ctrl+A and Ctrl+C on the list, and paste it here. Works for English and Spanish Google Maps (USA, UK, Spain, Mexico and elsewhere).

Any country, any city. Pick a country, then a state or division and a city or district, or type any city. Free, no key. OpenStreetMap has phones and websites but no ratings, and coverage varies by city, so check Google before you assume a business has no website. Works on the deployed site, not in the Claude preview.
