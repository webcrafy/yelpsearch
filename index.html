Yelp Lead Desk

Paste a Yelp business page, get a scored lead and a ready-to-send message.

Import leads

Choose where the data comes from. Every source fills the same preview list, and you add the businesses you want to the ledger.

Search Yelp for the businesses you want to sell websites to (plumbers, barbers, dentists), not for web designers. Paste the search link, open it, copy the results page text, and paste that text below. Each Yelp page lists about 10 businesses.

Option 1 · Google Places API

Returns name, phone, website, rating and reviews directly. You need an API key with Places API (New) enabled and a billing account in Google Cloud. Restrict the key to your site's address. This option works on the deployed site, not in the Claude preview.

Option 2 · Paste from Google Maps, no key

Search on Google Maps, press Ctrl+A and Ctrl+C on the results list, and paste it here. Fill the search box above with the same search so the city and category are set.

Free, no key and no card. OpenStreetMap lists many businesses with phone and website, but coverage is patchy and there are no ratings. If a business shows no website here, check Google before you assume it has none. This works on the deployed site, not in the Claude preview.

Use data from anywhere: Google Sheets, Excel, Outscraper, Apollo, or any CSV export. Paste the rows (first row is the column names) or load a .csv file. Columns like Name, Category, Phone, Website, Rating, Reviews, Address, City, State, Email are recognised automatically.

Add a lead

Open the business page on yelp.com, press Ctrl+A then Ctrl+C (Cmd on Mac), and paste it here.

Message

Pick a lead from the ledger.

Yelp does not show whether a number has WhatsApp. Look for a WhatsApp number on the business's own site or Google profile first. If there is none, use Facebook, Instagram or email. Emails need your real address in the footer.

Lead ledger

Score = website gap (35) + rating (15) + review count (20) + a known contact route (15) + claimed Yelp page (10) + years in business (5).
