Yelp Lead Desk

Paste a Yelp business page, get a scored lead and a ready-to-send message.

Import a Yelp search

Search Yelp for the businesses you want to sell websites to (plumbers, barbers, dentists), not for web designers. Paste the search link, then copy the results page text and paste it below. Each Yelp page lists about 10 businesses.

Add a lead

Open the business page on yelp.com, press Ctrl+A then Ctrl+C (Cmd on Mac), and paste it here.
